Privacy Policy

Effective 18 October 2026 · Global Altar – Prayer Hours (the “App”) is published by Tobi Omotayo, Ontario, Canada.

Global Altar is a Christian prayer app. We collect as little personal information as the App needs to work, we never sell it, and we never show advertising. This page explains what is collected, why, and the choices you have.

1. Using the App without an account

You can use most of the App as a guest: the prayer hours, Prayer Room, confessions, devotionals, memory verses and news. As a guest we do not collect your name or email. Prayers you save in “My Prayers” and your reminder settings are stored only on your own device.

2. Information you give us when you create an account

  • Email address and password – to sign you in. Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider (Supabase); we cannot read them.
  • Display name – shown next to prayer requests and group posts you choose to make with your profile (you can post anonymously instead).
  • Sign in with Apple (optional, iPhone) – if you use it, Apple gives us your name and email, or a private relay email if you choose to hide it.

3. Content you create

  • Prayer requests you post, and the “I just prayed” taps on them. Requests are visible to other users of the App; you can post anonymously, and each request expires on the date you choose.
  • Prayer groups you create or join, and messages you post on a group’s wall (visible to that group’s members).
  • Churches you follow.
  • Prayer sessions – when you join a prayer hour we record the hour and time (and your account if signed in) to show how many people are praying and your personal statistics.

4. Information collected automatically

We do not use analytics or tracking SDKs and we do not collect your precise location, contacts, photos or advertising identifiers. Like any internet service, our servers receive the IP address and basic device information needed to deliver content. The spoken confessions use your device’s built-in text-to-speech; nothing you hear is recorded.

5. Third-party services we rely on

  • Supabase – database, authentication and file storage (hosted on AWS infrastructure).
  • Expo – app builds and over-the-air updates.
  • rss2json – fetches public Christian news headlines for the News tab; it receives the request from your device.
  • YouTube and conference links – church live streams and prayer-line links open in your browser or that provider’s app and are governed by their privacy policies.
  • Apple App Store / Google Play – distribution and crash reports under their own policies.

We do not sell, rent or share your personal information with advertisers or data brokers.

6. How long we keep information

Account information and your content stay until you delete them or ask us to. Prayer requests are removed automatically after the expiry date you chose. Prayer-session records are kept to produce your statistics.

7. Your choices and rights — including deleting your account

  • Delete your account and data – email globalaltarapp@gmail.com from the address you signed up with and we will delete your account, requests, group posts and session history within 30 days. An in-app “Delete account” button is coming in a future update.
  • Edit or remove a prayer request or group post from within the App.
  • Notifications – prayer-hour reminders are local to your phone; turn them off in the App or in your phone’s settings.
  • Residents of Canada, the EU/UK and other regions may have rights to access, correct, export or erase their data – contact us and we will help.

8. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will remove it.

9. Security

Data travels encrypted (HTTPS) and is stored with access rules that only allow you to see your own private data. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the App.
